# Three-participant pilot study

## Purpose

The pilot checks feasibility, end-to-end behavior, usability, and failure modes.
It is not a population-level effectiveness study.

## Suggested protocol

1. Obtain informed consent for recording, landmark extraction, and research use.
2. Assign anonymous IDs (P001–P003); store the identity key separately.
3. Record camera setup, lighting, distance, warm-up, repetitions, and any injury.
4. Capture original video and synchronized system/session output.
5. Annotate technique phases independently from system predictions.
6. Run the predeclared rule-only and hybrid conditions on the same recordings.
7. Collect brief usability ratings and open comments.
8. Review failures and adverse/safety-related feedback.

The researcher may participate as an expert pilot subject, but this must be
declared. Avoid causal claims such as “improves performance” unless the study
design includes suitable controls and repeated pre/post measures.

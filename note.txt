Main meanings:
IDLE: visible but not performing or preparing.
PREPARATION: getting ready before the first technique state.
REP_START / REP_END: short repetition boundaries.
STEP_HOLD: intentionally holding a pose.
REP_RECOVERY: returning to the starting stance.
INCOMPLETE_REP: repetition started but never completed.
INCORRECT_MOVEMENT: movement does not match the selected technique.
__TRACKING_LOST__: landmarks are missing or unreliable.
__UNKNOWN__: the correct state cannot be confidently determined.
GUARD_TO_EXTENSION: movement leaving Guard and entering Extension.
Other transition labels receive equivalent automatic explanations.
